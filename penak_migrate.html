📋 How to get your ID Token

  1. Open your Penak app in the browser
  2. Log in with your account
  3. Press F12 to open DevTools
  4. Go to Console tab
  5. Paste this and press Enter:
    JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('penak_user')).idToken
  6. Copy the long token string and paste it above
